✨ Tips to Get Really Good at English

  1. Read 30 minutes a day — your brain learns new words like magic.
  2. Keep a word jar — when you see a new word, write it down with what you think it means.
  3. Re-read your favourite paragraphs — ask "why did the author say it like that?"
  4. Write a little every day — even one sentence about your day counts.
No XP. No badges. Just better thinking, one story at a time. 🌱
