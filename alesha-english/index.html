Recommended Reading

A Library for the Serious Young Reader

  • A Wrinkle in TimeL'Engle — physics, faith, and the strangeness of love.
  • The GiverLowry — memory, choice, and the price of comfort.
  • Bridge to TerabithiaPaterson — friendship and the architecture of grief.
  • Charlotte's WebWhite — mortality, devotion, and the dignity of small lives.
  • The Secret GardenBurnett — solitude, growth, and the will to bloom.
  • The Phantom TollboothJuster — wordplay, logic puzzles, and the joy of language.
  • Number the StarsLowry — quiet courage under occupation.
  • Where the Red Fern GrowsRawls — loyalty, loss, and the woods of childhood.
  • Anne of Green GablesMontgomery — imagination as moral instrument.
  • The Lion, the Witch & the WardrobeLewis — allegory and the geometry of right and wrong.

Habits of Serious Study

  1. Read thirty minutes daily — fiction, non-fiction, and one demanding text per week.
  2. Keep a vocabulary journal. Note the word, the sentence in which you met it, and a definition in your own words.
  3. Annotate as you read. Underline diction that surprises you and write a marginal question on every page.
  4. Write for fifteen minutes after each long passage — summary, reaction, or an imitation of the author's style.
